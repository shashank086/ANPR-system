About SmartFuel

The SmartFuel system is designed to enforce fuel restrictions based on vehicle age at petrol stations.

System Overview

The SmartFuel system installed at petrol stations is designed to enforce fuel restrictions based on the vehicle's age. The system uses advanced image processing and database management to ensure compliance with government regulations for pollution control.

Process Overview

The SmartFuel system determines a vehicle's age through a four-step process:

  1. License Plate Capture
    • SmartFuel cameras are installed at the entrance of petrol stations.
    • As a vehicle approaches, the camera captures a clear image of its license plate.
    • High-resolution cameras ensure accurate image capture under various lighting and weather conditions.
  2. Data Extraction
    • The captured license plate image is processed using Optical Character Recognition (OCR).
    • The OCR software converts the image into text, extracting the registration number.
    • This step ensures that even slightly damaged or dirty plates are recognized accurately.
  3. Database Cross-Referencing
    • The extracted registration number is matched against the government's Vahan Portal or any other authorized vehicle database.
    • The database contains essential details, including the vehicle's registration date, owner information, and compliance status.
    • This cross-referencing is performed in real time to minimize delays.
  4. Age Calculation
    • The system compares the vehicle's registration date with the current date.
    • If the vehicle exceeds the permissible age limit for fuel refueling (e.g., 10 years for diesel and 15 years for petrol), it triggers a Fuel Denial Alert.
    • The fuel station attendant is notified through an alert on their monitor, preventing the vehicle from refueling.

Technical Specifications

Camera System
  • High-resolution IP cameras

  • Weather-resistant housing
OCR Engine

Age Limits

  • Petrol vehicles: 15 years
Software Stack
